Good to know

A few handy details

Only the score is required

Strikes & spares are optional extras — add them when you want sharper stats.

Fat-fingered a score? Undo it

Tap Undo on the confirmation and it's gone from the server — no harm done.

Snap the lane monitor

Point your camera at the overhead score and it auto-fills the total — free, right on your phone.

Your PIN is yours

Lost it or got a new phone? Your captain resets your invite and you pick a fresh PIN.

Sign-in required to view

Scores aren't public — you sign in to see the squad, and only you can edit your own games.

It syncs for everyone

One shared, live set of numbers — what you log, your teammates and captain see straight away.
